# Rule: perf honesty

Applies to profiler, WS-*, JMH, MSPT claims.

- Cite the profiler signal that justifies the work
- Separate model/upper-bound benches from in-world same-run A/B
- Report method: population, flags, iterations, noise
- No silent trade of correctness for speed
- Fail-soft on optimization modules; yield to neighbors when required
