# Rule: parity gates

Applies when changing tick ownership, ordering, or concurrency.

- Assign equivalence class: E0 bit-identical, E1 semantic per-region, E2 documented interleave
- Gate exists before or with the feature it judges
- Prefer hard GameTests under weft-neoforge/src/gametest
- Chaos kill-save and neighbor postures are part of confidence, not optional flavor
- Known gaps must be written down (memory + RFC/README as appropriate)
- weft-parity outputs APPROVE or BLOCK; lead cannot redefine failure as success
